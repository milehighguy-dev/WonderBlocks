# Relay District

Open relay-district-preview.html for the overview, or index.html?demo=relay-district to edit and use Walk preview. The downloadable relay-district.building.json is a complete editable project.

Suggested mission: arrive at the cyan freight beacon, activate the amber archive console, and extract from the amber rooftop relay. These are layout markers; enemy AI, mission triggers and a win condition are not implemented by the scene.

- Direct route: freight court → wide Kamino approach → three-floor atrium → upper relay bridge → extraction roof.
- High flank: maintenance landing → exposed curved Kamino stairs → upper atrium entrance. Faster access to elevation, with less cover.
- Quiet loop: freight court → covered perimeter tunnel → curved maintenance room → west atrium door. Longer, sheltered traversal with short reveal points at its junctions.
- Secondary objective: first-floor atrium gallery → Bespin arched arcade → circular transit deck → Tantive corridor → divided archive. The archive spans two adjoining hulls. Its back door climbs to a tower overlook via an elevated entrance.

Cover crates sit off the central paths. The bridge and arcade are exposed lanes; room doors and divider openings are chokepoints. The two atrium stair flights occupy opposite sides, leaving a cross-floor circulation loop. The elevated spiral offers a route around the atrium stairwell chokepoints.

Systems used: editable hulls and modular crowns; ordinary and curved rooms; welded divider junctions; doors and windows; interiors cutting intersecting and adjoining hulls; multiple floors with route-based stairs and headroom holes; linked platform edges with railing gaps; rectangular, circular and capsule platforms; straight and curved routes; Kamino, Bespin, arched Bespin and Tantive styles; part texture finishes, props and player scale.

An additional ramp and public promenade connect the freight court to the eastern gallery without using the atrium stairs.

Footprint: approximately 110 × 120 metres; traversal elevations 0 / 3.2 / 6.4 metres; skyline towers up to 68 metres. 61 editable parts. The main app demo preserves the existing autosaved project. Use Save project to keep edits as a separate file.
