# Kapok preset

**Flora → Tree presets → Kapok · Mature Rainforest Tree** creates one editable
Ceiba pentandra. Its dimensions are in metres: approximately 40 m tall, a
37 × 32 m crown, and a bole about 2.3 m across above the root flare. This is a
representative large mature specimen, rather than a maximum-height tree.

The supplied photographs informed the long clear bole, irregular spreading
scaffold limbs, airy umbrella crown and tall narrow buttress walls. Six uneven
buttresses rise roughly 5.5–6.8 m and reach about 6–7 m from the tree centre.
These proportions are artistic choices within the range of large mature trees.

Botanical references:

- [Kew, Flora of Tropical East Africa description](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:1166232-2/general-information): trees 35–50 m, straight buttressed bole, horizontal main branches.
- [NYBG, Osa Peninsula tree account](https://sweetgum.nybg.org/science/projects/osa/monograph-details/?irn=53086): large trees can exceed 60 m and reach 3 m trunk diameter above the buttresses.
- [Smithsonian, forest canopy access](https://repository.si.edu/bitstream/handle/10088/22142/PineroEtAlManualBioDiversityEnglish.pdf): very tall kapok buttresses can prevent access even with a 5–7 m ladder.

The plant has 17 editable wood chains and 20 attached foliage groups. The roots
and bole use one continuous closed native profile, preventing any join between
them and preserving the thin walls when saved. The root flare is also available
separately as **Kapok Buttress Roots**
in **Bases**. The crown reuses the existing fine broadleaf cutouts; it does not
introduce a new species-specific leaf texture. Camera-facing foliage uses 2,000
triangles; the whole saved tree has about 18,000 triangles.

Place Kapok from **Flora → Tree presets → Kapok · Mature Rainforest Tree**.
Placement, joint editing, Undo/Redo, and project save/load are supported.
