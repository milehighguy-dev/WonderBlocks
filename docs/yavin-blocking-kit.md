# Yavin blocking kit

Open `index.html?demo=yavin`, or **Load example → Building kits → Yavin blocking workshop**. The **Yavin** library category contains eight choices. The example has sixteen objects in seven separate studies; it does not replace the current saved scene. `previews/yavin-kit-preview.html` provides orbit, player-height and clay views. The editable file is `examples/projects/yavin-blocking.building.json`.

| Choice | Purpose and controls |
| --- | --- |
| Arena gallery block | A repeatable upper fighting lane over a tapered lower through-passage, with back cover and a raised pier. Select it and open **Shape & sculpting** to adjust gallery width/depth, terrace height, underpass width/height and back-pier height. Dimensions are metres. |
| Arena stair bay | The gallery with two stair approaches, placed as an object group. The stairs remain ordinary editable routes; they meet the upper terrace at 4.2 m with 0.175 m risers. Edit the gallery and stairs separately when changing terrace height. |
| Temple passage route | Draw a route with battered walls, stepped shoulders and stone ribs. It uses the route editor, including turns, ramps and stairs. The default passage is 4 m wide and 4 m high. **Peek inside** is a preview cutaway; it does not remove the exported ceiling. |
| Large Yavin bridge | The complete broad Temple bridge matched to the supplied in-game screenshots: paved deck, stepped base curbs, tapered masonry side blocks, carved cap beams and solid transverse support slabs. The default is 10 m wide and 24 m long. Draw it as a route, including corners and curves. **Temple bridge sides** controls parapet height, cap thickness, block length/spacing/thickness. In **Material → Apply to**, choose **Bridge deck**, **Bridge structure**, **Side blocks**, **Cap beams** or **Base curbs** to change textures independently. |
| Aqueduct open span | The 18 m open trough section of the tall viaduct, with a substantial underside body of constant depth and low curbs. Clear walking width is 3.6 m. It has no roof or tall side walls. |
| Aqueduct covered span | The open span with a tapered stone cover added: same length, floor, curbs and underside. Ground fill is **off by default**. In **Aqueduct sections**, choose **Open span** or **Covered span**, and extend with **Add open span** / **Add covered span**. Enable **Fill aqueduct to ground** on a section to extend its support to world ground; moving or raising the route updates it automatically. This uses the route’s ground plane (normally Y = 0), rather than a fixed pier depth. Passage height and wall/roof thickness remain separate controls. Paving, body, curbs, passage walls and roof paint independently. |
| Stepped temple wall | Draw with the wall creator. It repeats tapered openings and stepped shoulders, with solid sills. Adjust opening width, height and spacing, wall thickness and wall height. It supports corners and curves. Window reveals can be painted separately. |
| Ruined temple wall | A solid wall with deeply broken masonry courses along its top, without windows. Draw in the wall creator or select **Yavin ruined wall** as the wall style. Height reaches 8 m; **Ruin span** changes the scale of the damage up to 16 m. Thickness, curves and corners remain editable. The damage is stable as the wall extends; trimmed ends meet adjoining wall sections at a shared height. |

Eight architectural finishes appear under the **Yavin** material theme: temple stone, stone floor, weathered wall, Temple bridge paving, bridge stone, carved bridge cap, aqueduct masonry and aqueduct paving. All pieces use the standard project save, material and SWBF2 mesh/collision export paths. Gallery underpasses remain clear in movement collision. The kit has not been installed or playtested in the game.

## Source inspection and selection

The actual BF2 Temple world (`assets/worlds/YAV/world1/yavin1.wld`) and original BF1 Arena world and building layer (`Yavin/world2/yav2.WLD`, `yav2_Buildings01.lyr`) were inspected locally. Twenty-three mesh references were decoded and compared with the existing tools; source hashes, dimensions and triangle counts are recorded in `assets/yavin/sources.json`.

| Source architecture | Reused idea |
| --- | --- |
| BF1 `yav2_bldg_arena_straight_1` and `_2` | Compress their large arena galleries into repeatable blocks with lower passages and upper fighting lanes. |
| BF1 `yav2_bldg_arena_tunnel`, `door_ruin_8`; BF2 great-temple interior | A splayed passage section with stepped shoulders, extended through the existing editable route system. |
| BF1 `yav2_bldg_window_ruin_16`, `door_ruin_8` | Trapezoidal openings, solid sills and stepped wall tops, made into a wall-creator style. |
| BF1 `yav2_bldg_wall_ruin_16` | Deep losses between solid masonry remnants, with sloping fracture edges and broken courses. This supplies the separate solid ruined-wall style. |
| BF2 `yav_bldg_aquaduct` | The broad bridge with carved cap beams, tapered side blocks, raised curbs and continuous transverse support slabs. Its support web is solid across the width, with flared crowns, base bands and bevelled feet. Passages run between slabs. |
| BF2 `yav_bldg_ruins_wall1` and `wall2` | The tall aqueduct from the later screenshots. A 12 m enclosed pier and an 18 m open span share a 3.5358 m stock walking width. The workshop alternates three piers and two spans in a single 72 m route, with its floor 20.8 m above ground. |
| BF1 `yav2_bldg_Arch_stairs`; BF2 main and side temple stairs | Paired approaches and usable terrace connections, authored with the existing stair tool. |

These are new procedural blocking forms inspired by those structures, rather than whole stock buildings placed as props. Pyramids, ordinary arches, columns, plain wall slabs and small decoration were excluded because existing tools already cover them. The stair bay deliberately reuses native stairs rather than adding another staircase shape.

The eight 128×128 finishes use local Mod Tools texture sheets. The aqueduct uses `yav_bldg_greattemple.tga` masonry and `yav2_bldg_floor2.tga` paving. The broad bridge uses `yav_main_6.tga` paving and crops from `yav_bldg_aquaduct.tga` for stone and carved caps. Source paths, hashes and crop rectangles are in `assets/yavin/sources.json`. Original SDK files are unchanged. These game textures retain their original ownership.
## Verification

The latest correction passed 129 focused checks covering Yavin geometry, ordinary and themed hallways, route curves/turns, material finishes, native collision/export, navigation barriers and Mos Eisley entries. The aqueduct checks trace a soldier-height passage through every open/enclosed boundary, sample the continuous floor, verify roofs only over enclosed sections, and check solid pier walls, low-curb collision and open sky. Straight, curved, ramp and corner variants have closed faces and retain independent roof materials through saving. The five-section example has fewer than 700 triangles. Covered defaults have the same underside as open spans. Optional fill was checked at different elevations, after moving, on ramps, curves, turns and scaled/rotated sections. Filled supports stop at ground, keep closed faces and retain texture density when repainted. Adjacent filled sections join through turns without overlapping footings.

Broad-bridge checks verify that the full width of each support slab blocks movement and projectiles, while transverse paths between slabs remain clear. Splitting a span preserves support spacing. The broad bridge retains closed straight/corner/curved solids, protective side collision, projectile gaps between side blocks, continuous carved cap UVs and independently paintable components. The default complete bridge remains below 1,000 triangles.

Isolated browser checks verified all eight library choices, drawing a covered span with ground fill off, enabling/disabling the aqueduct-specific fill, undo/redo, appending both section types, changing enclosure, painting roofs independently, and saving/reopening after height changes. Broad-bridge drawing, side-block controls and independent deck/block/cap painting also passed. Orbit, player-height, cutaway and underside screenshots were compared with the supplied references and the stock meshes. The user's open scene was preserved. No in-game playtest was performed.

Floating spans now keep a constant underside through each flat/ramp join; repeated section-end haunches and the obsolete underside-depth setting were removed. Masonry uses route distance and face-profile coordinates, so courses follow rising/descending grades and meet at section boundaries. Roof, wall, curb and underside alignment are checked at multiple texture scales and after repainting. The floating-route and covered-route screenshots reproduce the user’s flat/descent/flat/descent layout.

The floating body reaches 2.1 m below the walking surface by default, continuing the original open span’s middle depth through its ends. Removing end haunches does not reduce it to a thin slab. A focused 46-check follow-up verifies the restored body, constant depth at every station, matched ramp/flat joins, UV alignment, fill behavior and native collision.
