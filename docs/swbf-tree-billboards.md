# Yavin, Dagobah and Endor foliage

**Audience:** Contributors and coding agents. Historical research and asset notes, not a user guide; details may no longer match the current app.

Inspected the local SWBF2 Mod Tools authoring meshes and their object definitions in Blender. The original wood meshes remain available in a separate reference scene. The Wonderblocks example recreates wood with editable native Flora branch profiles; it is a shape reconstruction rather than a verbatim import of the original wood geometry.

The living trees attach `ClassLabel = "leafpatch"` objects to `hp_branch...` hardpoints. These ODFs provide the particle count, spread radius, height, size range, card aspect and darkness range. The placeholder `hoth_prop_grass.msh` in the leafpatch ODF is not a modeled canopy. Their native 256 × 256 cutout textures contain a 2 × 2 atlas of leaf or vine shapes. Wonderblocks chooses a stable randomized tile for each card rather than displaying the entire atlas on every particle.

`src/flora/flora-billboards.js` implements seed-stable distributions, atlas selection, alpha cutouts and camera-facing vertex deformation. The same draw receives the current camera orientation, so the editor's on-demand renderer has no delayed frame. CPU posing is only needed for picking and static geometry export. Picking samples the alpha mask so transparent areas do not block wood selection. Foliage does not participate in walking collision or cast rectangular shadow slabs. Conservative bounds keep the complete canopy visible while orbiting. Parent transforms, plant mirroring and uniform scaling retain camera facing.

Individual foliage parts start at editor-sized dimensions. The Flora palette’s **Tree presets** group saves Yavin Temple Broadleaf, Yavin Slender Grove Tree, Dagobah Grand Swamp Tree, Dagobah Twisted Elder, Endor Forest Redwood and Endor Woodland Tree as complete editable plants. Drag a preset onto the ground to create a new tree; its wood joints, attached foliage, bark finish and leafpatch settings remain editable. Each placement gets fresh joint and organ IDs, and creation supports Undo/Redo and save/load. The example uses measured crown anchors and source-sized leafpatch settings; its two representative Yavin trees, two Dagobah trees, two Endor trees and hanging vines contain 380 camera-facing cards plus 33 fixed Endor leaf groups, totaling 1,420 foliage triangles. This is a reconstruction of the visual technique, not a port of SWBF2's particle-engine code. Falling leaves and scattering birds are not implemented.

The `.building.json` retains editable joints and all foliage settings. The Blender reconstruction contains the wood actually generated by Wonderblocks, packed textures, native atlas UVs and camera constraints for its cards. Camera-facing clusters copy the display camera's rotation; upright vines turn toward it while retaining vertical orientation. Move the Blender display camera to demonstrate the effect. Blender's arbitrary perspective viewport does not drive those constraints. Geometry-only OBJ exports freeze the active Wonderblocks view and do not include materials or dynamic billboard behavior.

New plants and all Tree presets default to SWBF2 surface detail when placed, including the botanical and Felucia presets. Previously saved plants keep their chosen detail setting. In the Flora editor, **Surface detail → SWBF2** reduces the complete joined wood surface to a game-sized triangle budget. It samples authored branching fields around their individual limbs before reducing polygons, retains thin-limb reinforcement for imported solid branches, then uses topology-preserving edge collapse with a bounded surface error. It does not lower the sampling grid further, remove leaf groups, or change editable joints and attachments. Boundaries stay fixed; connected limbs stay connected. Shape accuracy takes priority over the target budget, so complex roots can keep extra faces. The choice is saved per plant, supports Undo/Redo, and is shared by the worker preview, finished scene and exports. Existing Low and Standard plants retain their original geometry. Native textured surface parts and foliage are left intact.

For the six reference presets, measured wood triangles before/after selecting SWBF2 are: Yavin Temple Broadleaf 4,660 → 804; Yavin Slender Grove 5,332 → 566; Dagobah Grand Swamp 6,272 → 1,052; Dagobah Twisted Elder 4,660 → 1,160; Endor Forest Redwood 3,192 → 276; Endor Woodland 3,664 → 518. These are reconstructed shapes rather than exact stock meshes. Foliage is unchanged at 1,420 triangles across the scene, giving **5,796 total visible triangles** instead of 29,200. Geometry checks verify closed surfaces, component counts, volume, overall bounds and a long thin attached limb. The editor's triangle count uses visible geometry and updates when the joined worker surface arrives.

Endor Forest Redwood recreates the roughly 39-metre `END/msh/end_prop_tree01.msh` with editable trunk joints and roots. Its 33 attached leaf groups form eleven overlapping tiers directly on the trunk, without modeled wooden boughs. The broad canopy tapers continuously to a leafy tip while leaving the lower trunk exposed. Its needle cutout comes from the right half of `end_prop_tree_combi01.tga` (the original branch mesh uses U=0.5–1); the bare-branch half is excluded. This is one cutout, not a 2 × 2 atlas. Endor Woodland Tree follows `end_prop_leaftree3.msh` and its three `hp_branch_7`, `hp_branch_17` and `hp_branch_18` crown anchors. Its three leafpatches use the native `end_leaf2` atlas and `end_prop_leafpatch2.odf` settings: 20 cards, radius 10, height 2, width 5–6 and aspect 0.5. Wood is reconstructed with Flora, rather than imported as an uneditable mesh.

**Endor Redwood Foliage** is a reusable part under **Leaves**, made from five native folded sprays sharing a single attachment point (20 triangles). The preset uses these groups directly on the trunk, including its terminal joint. **Endor Redwood Spray** remains available as a smaller individual part under **Leaves**. It retains one four-triangle folded section of the original `branch_bush` geometry, with its origin at the native stem pixel and the original UVs remapped to the foliage half of the texture. These planes stay fixed to their attachment; they are not camera-facing billboards. The existing Needle Bough billboard remains available as a separate option.

The texture name was confirmed directly from the `end_prop_tree01.msh` strings. The PC game texture was independently recovered from a workspace copy of the installed `GameData/data/_lvl_pc/end/end1.lvl` using swbf-unmunge v1.3.0 with SWBF II / PC settings. The new spray uses that recovered texture's original colors and alpha cutout. Generic hash warnings during level extraction did not prevent recovery of this named model and texture. The branch planes come from the matching Mod Tools authoring mesh. Whole-tree arrangement is reconstructed in Flora rather than imported as a rigid mesh. Browser checks cover preset placement, native leaf dragging onto bark, joint propagation, creation and leaf Undo/Redo, Finish and reopening.

Preset scale is measured in Wonderblocks metres, against the original model coordinates and the existing roughly two-metre clone trooper reference. Walk preview changes the viewpoint without scaling the scene. The raw Yavin ODF dimensions were verified against the shipped PC `yav/yav1.lvl`, rather than assuming the Mod Tools files were different.

The labeled Mod Tools executable confirms another scale step: `LeafPatch::LeafPatch` at `0x004b88c0` assigns each leafpatch instance a shared random contribution of 0–0.2 plus an independent 0–0.1 contribution per axis, added to 0.7. The latter coefficient was read as 0.1 at `0x00a2c074`. Whole-tree presets now save stable scales within this 0.7–1.0 range, rather than placing every cluster at full size. The raw card-size and spread controls retain their original units. This reduces the reconstructed Yavin canopy widths from about 39/27 metres to 34/21 metres; source-height trunks remain tall. It does not claim to reproduce the game's complete random sequence or leaf animation.

The Felucia Bladder Pair and Scarlet Bloom were also oversized relative to their source models. Their complete attached plants now reach about 5.25 and 4.86 metres above ground, respectively. Their stems and root-level organs were scaled together; petals inherit their calyx’s scale. Surface pins, editable joints and saved attachment relationships remain intact. Browser checks use actual tree-preset placement, joint dragging, Undo/Redo, Finish/reopening and ground-level walking in an isolated scene.

Aspen summer and fall now recolour the stock `yav_leaf` atlas directly. Their 256 × 256 alpha masks and four atlas tiles are byte-identical to Yavin; only RGB changes. Each tree uses eleven attached patches of twelve broad cards (132 cards / 264 foliage triangles), replacing the former 726-card sprays. Crown size stays within the existing metre-scale bounds. Winter removes the fine twig-fan decorations and secondary fine forks, retaining the twelve primary trunk/bough branches. The optional twig asset remains available for previously saved projects. Seasonal preset thumbnails are rebuilt from the current geometry.

The local leafpatch audit compared the five Yavin/Dagobah/Endor atlas buffers with their source Mod Tools TGAs: all RGBA bytes match. The main full-tree canopy settings match their ODF particle count, radius, height, size, aspect and darkness. Twisted Elder retains its intentionally smaller canopy/vine distribution to fit that reconstructed shape; the per-instance 0.7–1.0 scale variation remains unchanged.
